## 1. The Four Pillar Criteria of IELTS Writing

Examiners evaluate every Writing submission using **4 equally weighted criteria (25% each)**:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               IELTS WRITING SCORE ARCHITECTURE              │
├──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┬────────────────┤
│    25% TR/TA │       25% CC │       25% LR │        25% GRA │
│ Task Response│ Coherence &  │   Lexical    │  Grammatical   │
│/ Achievement │   Cohesion   │   Resource   │Range & Accuracy│
└──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┴────────────────┘
```

### 1. Task Achievement (TA) / Task Response (TR)
* **Task 1 (Task Achievement):** Did you objectively satisfy all parts of the prompt?
  * Provide a clear, comprehensive **Overview** highlighting major trends, exceptions, or stages.
  * Accurately report and support key data features **without inserting personal assumptions**.
* **Task 2 (Task Response):** Did you thoroughly address every prompt nuance?
  * Present a clear, unequivocal **Thesis Statement** and position throughout the response.
  * Fully develop, extend, and support main ideas with logical evidence (PEEL framework).

### 2. Coherence and Cohesion (CC)
* **Coherence (Logical Thought Flow):** Are ideas grouped logically into well-defined paragraphs?
* **Cohesion (Linking & Thematic Progression):** Do sentences connect naturally using cohesive devices, referential pronouns, and substitution without appearing mechanical or repetitive?

### 3. Lexical Resource (LR)
* Breadth and precision of academic vocabulary (Academic Word List - AWL).
* Natural use of topic-specific collocations and nuanced word choice.
* Ability to paraphrase concepts accurately with minimal orthographic (spelling) errors.

### 4. Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA)
* Variety of syntactic structures: Complex, compound, conditional, passive, and inverted sentences.
* Proportion of error-free sentences (Band 7.0+ requires frequent error-free sentences).
* Punctuation precision (avoiding comma splices and run-on sentences).

## 2. Band Descriptors Matrix: Band 6.0 vs Band 7.0 vs Band 8.5+

| Criterion | Band 6.0 (Competent) | Band 7.0 (Good) | Band 8.5+ (Very Good / Expert) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TR / TA** | Addresses all parts but some aspects are insufficiently developed; Overview is present but may lack clarity. | Fully addresses all parts; Clear position throughout; Well-developed response with relevant supporting ideas. | Fully satisfies all requirements; Insightful, sophisticated perspective; Flawlessly supported arguments. |
| **CC** | Arranges information coherently; uses linking words but with some mechanical overuse or faulty referencing. | Logically organizes ideas with clear progression; skillful use of diverse cohesive devices; clear central topic per paragraph. | Seamless flow; thematic progression without drawing attention to mechanical connectives; flawless paragraphing. |
| **LR** | Adequate range for the task; attempts less common words but with occasional inaccuracies in collocation or word choice. | Sufficient range of vocabulary to allow flexibility and precision; uses less common collocations with minor slips. | Wide, sophisticated vocabulary; natural, idiomatic mastery of collocations; negligible rare minor slips. |
| **GRA** | Mix of simple and complex sentence forms; makes frequent minor grammar errors, though meaning remains clear. | Produces frequent error-free sentences; good control of complex syntax; occasional non-systematic errors. | Wide range of structures used with full flexibility and accuracy; rare minor errors characteristic of native speakers. |

## 3. The 3 Golden Rules for Instant Band Improvement
1. **Never skip the Task 1 Overview:** Omitting an overview automatically caps your Task 1 score at **Band 5.0 in Task Achievement**, regardless of how advanced your vocabulary is.
2. **Eliminate Punctuation Comma Splices:** Joining two independent clauses with a simple comma instead of a period, semicolon, or coordinating conjunction prevents your score from reaching Band 7.0 in GRA.
3. **Prioritize Precision over Obscure Vocabulary:** Using an overly complex word incorrectly damages both your LR and CC scores. Choose the most precise academic collocation for the specific context.
