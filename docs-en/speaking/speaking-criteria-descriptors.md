## 1. The 4 Official Speaking Assessment Criteria

Examiners grade your performance across **4 equally weighted dimensions (25% each)**:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               IELTS SPEAKING SCORE ARCHITECTURE             │
├──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┬────────────────┤
│       25% FC │       25% LR │      25% GRA │        25% PR  │
│  Fluency &   │   Lexical    │  Grammatical │ Pronunciation  │
│  Coherence   │   Resource   │Range & Accur.│                │
└──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┴────────────────┘
```

### 1. Fluency and Coherence (FC)
* **Speech Continuity:** Ability to maintain a natural flow of speech without unnatural hesitation or excessive mid-sentence pauses to search for grammar or vocabulary.
* **Coherence & Discourse Structure:** Connecting thoughts logically with topic development and natural fillers (`"Well, to be perfectly honest..."`) instead of silent gaps.

### 2. Lexical Resource (LR)
* Breadth, variety, and precision of colloquial expressions, idiomatic turns of phrase, and phrasal verbs.
* Ability to paraphrase concepts when a precise term escapes you.
* Natural avoidance of rigid, clunky academic jargon unsuited to spoken English.

### 3. Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA)
* Balanced mix of simple and complex sentences (conditionals, relative clauses, concessive conjunctions).
* Consistent grammatical accuracy (subject-verb agreement, tense consistency, and correct pronoun usage).

### 4. Pronunciation (PR)
* The four pillars of natural pronunciation:
  1. **Individual Sounds & Minimal Pairs:** Clear consonant clusters and distinct vowel lengths.
  2. **Word Stress:** Correct primary and secondary syllable stress.
  3. **Sentence Stress & Rhythm:** Stressing content words while reducing function words.
  4. **Intonation:** Expressive pitch contours reflecting emotion, emphasis, and intent.

## 2. Band Progression Descriptors Matrix

| Criterion | Band 5.0 – 5.5 | Band 6.0 – 6.5 | Band 7.5 – 8.5+ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **FC** | Slow speech rate with noticeable pauses; frequent self-correction; relies heavily on basic linkers. | Willing to speak at length; may lose coherence due to occasional hesitation or repetition; uses a range of connectives. | Speaks fluently with rare hesitations for idea formulation; develops topics coherently and comprehensively. |
| **LR** | Limited vocabulary range; struggles with topic-specific discussions; frequent word choice errors. | Wide enough range to discuss topics at length; uses some idiomatic language though with occasional inappropriate choices. | Uses wide, flexible vocabulary; naturally introduces rare idioms and colloquialisms with subtle precision. |
| **GRA** | Produces basic sentence forms accurately; limited complex syntax with frequent grammatical errors. | Uses a mix of simple and complex forms; errors in complex structures do not impede general comprehension. | Consistently produces a wide range of complex structures with high accuracy; errors are rare, non-systematic slips. |
| **PR** | Pronunciation errors occasionally cause listener strain; flat intonation and inconsistent syllable stress. | Generally intelligible throughout; uses a mix of phonological features with occasional lapses. | Uses a full range of phonological features with subtle nuance and effortless intelligibility throughout. |
