## 1. Vì sao so sánh là "xương sống" của Task 1?

Đề Task 1 luôn yêu cầu: *"Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and **make comparisons where relevant**."* Bài chỉ liệt kê số liệu mà không so sánh thường bị giới hạn điểm Task Achievement.

## 2. So sánh hơn & so sánh nhất

| Loại tính từ | So sánh hơn | So sánh nhất |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 âm tiết | high → **higher** than | **the highest** |
| 2 âm tiết tận cùng -y | busy → **busier** than | **the busiest** |
| 2+ âm tiết | popular → **more popular** than | **the most popular** |
| Bất quy tắc | good → **better**; bad → **worse**; far → **further** | **the best**; **the worst**; **the furthest** |
| Ít hơn | **less** popular than / **fewer** cars than | **the least** popular / **the fewest** cars |

> ⚠️ **Fewer** + danh từ đếm được số nhiều (*fewer students*). **Less** + danh từ không đếm được (*less money, less time*).

Ví dụ Task 1:
- *Coal was **the most important** energy source in 1980, but it became **less significant than** gas by 2010.*
- *Japan had **the lowest** unemployment rate of the five countries.*

---

## 3. Bổ nghĩa cho so sánh — mức độ chênh lệch

Đây là chỗ học viên Band 7 khác biệt: **nói rõ chênh lệch lớn hay nhỏ**.

| Mức độ | Từ bổ nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Rất lớn | **far, much, considerably, significantly, substantially** | *Spending on health was **considerably higher** in the US.* |
| Nhỏ | **slightly, a little, marginally** | *Women's earnings were **slightly lower** than men's.* |
| Nhấn mạnh so sánh nhất | **by far** | *Tourism was **by far the largest** source of income.* |

> ❌ *very higher* → ✅ *much higher / far higher*

## 4. So sánh bằng & so sánh gấp bội

| Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **as + adj/adv + as** | bằng | *Rail travel is **as fast as** flying on short routes.* |
| **not as/so + adj + as** | không bằng | *Rural areas are **not as** densely populated **as** cities.* |
| **twice / three times + as + adj + as** | gấp 2/3 lần | *The figure for Canada was **twice as high as** that for Mexico.* |
| **X times + more/higher + than** | gấp X lần | *Car ownership was **three times higher than** in 1990.* |
| **half as + adj + as** | bằng một nửa | *Spending on food was **half as much as** spending on housing.* |
| **the same + noun + as** | giống | *Both cities had **the same population as** each other in 2000.* |
| **similar to / different from** | tương tự / khác | *The trend for wind power was **similar to** that for solar.* |

> 💡 **Tránh lặp danh từ bằng "that of / those of":**
> *The population of Tokyo is larger than **that of** London.* (that = the population)
> *Prices in 2020 were higher than **those in** 2010.* (those = prices)

---

## 5. So sánh kép — "Càng… càng…"

**Công thức:** `The + so sánh hơn + S + V, the + so sánh hơn + S + V`

- ***The more** people use cars, **the worse** air quality becomes.*
  (Càng nhiều người dùng ô tô, chất lượng không khí càng tệ.)
- ***The earlier** children start learning a language, **the more easily** they acquire it.*

**Tăng dần liên tục:** `so sánh hơn + and + so sánh hơn`
- *Housing is becoming **more and more** expensive.*
- *The gap grew **wider and wider**.*

---

## 6. Cấu trúc đối chiếu hai đối tượng (rất hay dùng trong Task 1)

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
| :--- | :--- |
| **while / whereas** | *Sales of laptops rose, **whereas** sales of desktops fell.* |
| **compared with / to** | ***Compared with** 2010, unemployment was much lower in 2020.* |
| **in contrast / by contrast** | *Germany relied heavily on coal. **By contrast**, France relied on nuclear power.* |
| **unlike** + danh từ | ***Unlike** Italy, Spain saw a steady increase in tourists.* |
| **the former … the latter** | *Cars and buses were studied; **the former** was preferred by most commuters, **the latter** by students.* |
| **respectively** | *The figures for men and women were 40% and 35% **respectively**.* |

---

## 7. Lỗi hay gặp

| ❌ Sai | ✅ Đúng |
| :--- | :--- |
| *more higher* | *higher* |
| *the most cheapest* | *the cheapest* |
| *London is bigger **as** Paris.* | *London is bigger **than** Paris.* |
| *The salary of doctors is higher than nurses.* | *The salary of doctors is higher than **that of** nurses.* |
| *less people* | *fewer people* |

---

## 8. Bài tập nhanh

1. Bảng: Nam 60%, Nữ 30%. Viết 1 câu dùng *twice as … as*.
2. Viết câu "Càng đọc nhiều, vốn từ càng rộng."
3. Sửa lỗi: *The number of cars in 2020 was more higher than 2000.*

<details>
<summary>Xem đáp án</summary>

1. *The proportion of men was twice as high as that of women.*
2. *The more you read, the wider your vocabulary becomes.*
3. *The number of cars in 2020 was much higher than that in 2000.*

</details>
